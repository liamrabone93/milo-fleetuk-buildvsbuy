The honest comparisonBuild in-house, or run Milo.

You were straight with me on Friday, so here is the same back. Where building in-house genuinely wins

Total control of the roadmap, an asset that sits on your own balance sheet, no vendor in the middle, and data that never leaves your estate. If the strategic goal is to own an AI capability rather than to answer questions about DPF failures, that is a real argument and no comparison table beats it.

Two things are worth weighing against it. The first is that unless you self-host a model, which is a far bigger project than the one being discussed, a bespoke build still sends your data to a third-party model API, so you take on the security posture and the audit trail yourselves rather than removing them. The second is that the IP worth owning here is probably not the plumbing. Nobody gains an advantage from owning a Navigator connector. The advantage is in knowing which questions matter and how Fleet UK defines the answers, and that stays yours on either route.

The one line

The two are not mutually exclusive.

You said running the trial while planning to hire would feel disingenuous. I would argue the opposite. Recruitment takes months either way, and six weeks of this tells you which questions the business actually asks, where the data quality falls over, and what good looks like. On the route where you build, that is a written specification you hand your new hire on day one instead of asking them to work it out blind. It is the most useful thing you could do for a build you have not started yet.
