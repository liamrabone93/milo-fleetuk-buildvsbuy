None of it is a first attempt. Two years, around a million in investment and 700+ integrations already shipped. The real asset is knowing which questions matter and how Fleet UK defines the answers, and that stays yours either way.

Bespoke is the part we do with you, in week one. Bespoke here means your definitions, your thresholds and your vulnerable-customer flags, changed in an afternoon. It does not have to mean a year of somebody writing code.
